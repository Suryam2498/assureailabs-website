Frequently asked questions

Quick answers before you get in touch.

What kind of work can you automate?

Repetitive tasks such as report building, Excel and file processing, collecting data from public websites, browser tasks and scheduled jobs. If you do it by hand more than a few times a week, it is a good candidate.

I run a small business. Is this for me?

Yes. Small, clearly scoped tasks are a good place to start. We work with businesses of any size.

What do you need from me to give a quote?

A short description of the task, a sample file or link, and the output you want. We reply with scope, price and timeline before any work starts.

How do you price?

Fixed price for small tasks, hourly or a monthly retainer for ongoing work. You always see the scope and price in writing first.

Is my data safe?

Your files and data stay yours and are used only for your project. We are happy to sign an NDA, and we only work with public data or data you are allowed to use.

Do you also test software?

Yes. Test automation (Playwright, Selenium), performance and security testing, and evaluation of AI chatbots and RAG apps are all part of what we do.

What happens after delivery?

You get documented code and a short usage guide, plus one round of fixes. Ongoing support is available if you want it.
